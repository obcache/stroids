# Image generation provenance

Built-in `image_gen.imagegen` was used, without an API key or CLI fallback.
The selected concept is `docs/design/stroids-concept.png`. The selected production
sprite atlas is `public/art/sprites.png`. Intermediate discarded art is not part
of the project. UI text/controls/HUD and mechanics remain native code.

## Selected concept prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one complete polished desktop arcade game application screen for STROIDS, an original respectful homage to classic asteroid shooting games. Landscape 1536x1024 screenshot, flat front-on UI, no photographed monitor. Whole application fits in frame. Distinctive sophisticated vector arcade art direction: near-black charcoal #10120f background, thin muted olive borders, warm ivory text #eeeee4, acid yellow #d9fa68 accents, monospace tiny utility text and wide heavy geometric sans headings. Topmost thin custom desktop titlebar: small angular ship mark and STROIDS left, minimize/maximize/close controls right. Below, roomy header with STROIDS wordmark left and 'HOW TO PLAY' 'FLIGHT LOG' 'SETTINGS' right. Main content within 54px page gutters. Large thin-bordered game arena taking most of screen, ratio roughly 1.9:1. Arena has its own top HUD SCORE 000000 left, WAVE 01 center, three small ship outlines right. Sparse starfield, original irregular thin ivory outlined drifting asteroids, small yellow vector spaceship at center-right with yellow engine flame, faint sparse olive navigational grid and central crosshair. Menu at left of arena, no backdrop panel: large two-line heading 'A little ship.' then 'A lot of space.' in ivory geometric sans, body 'Drift. Dodge. Break things.' and next line 'One more run starts here.' in muted gray. Bright acid yellow rectangular button 'LAUNCH RUN' with small up-right arrow, under it monospace 'or press ENTER'. Asteroids placed mostly right and outer edges so text remains readable. Bottom strip within arena has 'SECTOR 01 / THE QUIET BELT' left and 'ALL SYSTEMS READY' with yellow indicator dot right. Below arena clean horizontal control legend on same page background: left 'BUILT FOR THE LONG WAY HOME.' small muted text; right boxed arrow keys and text 'ROTATE' 'THRUST' boxed SPACE and 'FIRE' boxed P and 'PAUSE'. Bottommost small footer 'An independent tribute to the golden age of arcades.' left and 'LOCAL PLAY / NO QUARTERS REQUIRED' right. No cards, no badges, no marketing sections, no navigation sidebar, no gradients. All real UI texts, HUD, controls will be HTML and game art separately animated. Original ship/rocks, no Atari logos or copied artwork. Include whole full screen, generous whitespace, high contrast, beautifully restrained professional indie game interface.

## Selected production atlas prompt

Create an extremely clean vector-style 2x2 sprite atlas, SQUARE image, on perfectly solid pure black #000000 background. Exactly four separate objects each centered in their own equal square quadrant, no object crosses the center horizontal or vertical boundaries. Top-left: hollow angular irregular asteroid with 12 sides, thin ivory outline, three tiny thin internal crack lines, perfectly BLACK interior. Top-right: different hollow angular irregular asteroid with 11 sides, thin ivory outline, two tiny internal crack lines, perfectly BLACK interior. Bottom-left: another hollow angular irregular asteroid with 10 sides, thin ivory outline, perfectly BLACK interior. Bottom-right: hollow acid-yellow outlined three-point dart spacecraft, symmetric and pointing UP, perfectly BLACK interior, simple geometric profile with two swept wings and notched tail, one thin cockpit line. All objects fit within 75% of their quadrant, centered. Lines must be thin and sharp with uniform stroke weight. Objects have NO FILLED AREAS, NO TEXTURE, NO NOISE, NO DISTRESSING, NO SPECKLES, NO SHADING, NO GLOW. Only razor-sharp sparse ivory or yellow lines on flat black background. 1980s arcade oscilloscope line art. No text. Production clean digital vector style, not a sketch.

The first transparent atlas draft had unwanted texture and was rejected. The
selected flat black version is rendered with additive compositing so its black
background does not obscure the code-rendered field. Raster cell boundaries are
derived from the actual atlas dimensions, not an assumed generation size.
